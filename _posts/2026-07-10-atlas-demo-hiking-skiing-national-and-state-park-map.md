[Open the interactive Atlas map](/apps/atlas/)

An app I built that shows all my Alltrails, Slopes data on a MapLibre map. Also shows Colorado 14ers, 13ers, and a number of different boundaries including: state line boundaries, national park boundaries, and state/local parks.

Can zoom to the selected activity and style boundaries by the number of activities. Has multiple ways to filter and search activities and boundaries.
