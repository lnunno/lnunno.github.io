[Open Lucas' Bookshelf](/apps/books/)

An app I built that visualizes books read, want to read with a number of different filters. Has a book page view that shows all highlights, rating, genre and subgenres, taste role.

Shows reading trends in two different charts with different visualizations to scale them based on number of highlights and show the time spent reading, as well as monthly stats as a heatmap with different selectable color keys.
