I've been using [Visualping](https://visualping.io/) to detect new books being added to [Netgalley](https://www.netgalley.com/) for advanced copies of books from my favorite authors and found it to be really helpful. Instead of checking every day or whenever I remember, it instead will email when the page updates, letting me focus on other things but still be up to date when I can read an advanced copy from a favorite author.

![alt text](/assets/images/visualping-2.png)
