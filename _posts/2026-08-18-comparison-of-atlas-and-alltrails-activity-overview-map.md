A quick one, but I wanted to just post a comparison of AllTrail's activity overview map and my app, Atlas. This is the [AllTrails one](https://www.alltrails.com/explore/members/lucas-nunno/recordings):
![AllTrails activity overview map of Colorado, showing recorded activities as numbered clusters and individual tracks](/assets/images/posts/alltrails-activity-overview-map.png)

[Atlas](https://www.lucasnunno.com/apps/atlas/?overlays=alltrails-activities%2Cus-national-parks%2Ccolorado-state-parks%2Ccolorado-open-spaces):
![Atlas activity overview map of Colorado, showing all 146 activities as individual tracks alongside national parks, state parks, and open spaces](/assets/images/posts/atlas-activity-overview-map.png)

This reminds me of [this blog](https://blog.greg.technology/2026/06/12/map-clustering-is-not-my-favorite.html) [his example map](https://gregsadetsky.github.io/clustering-is-not-my-favorite/?map=densitydots) I saw on HackerNews a couple of months ago; just show the points! The clustering makes it way less useful. To actually show the activity track you have to hover over, it lags a few seconds and then eventually shows it to you. It's sort of a weird page overall and I could only really find it on desktop, not sure if it exists on mobile or not. This is one of my big uses of tracking hikes though, I want to see where I've been even when I'm zoomed out so I can "fill in more of the map" on my next hike and go somewhere new. This is hard to do using AllTrails.
