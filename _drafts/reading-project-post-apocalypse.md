## The Passage (read)

A lot of the story deals with the actual post apocalyptic event at least in the first book but most of the series looks into how to rebuild after the vampire apocalypse.

This one is unique because it does also have a lot of time skips and focuses on using the remnants of the pre-apocalypse world but also the question of whether or not it's safe to rebuild society and the cycles there of build up and collapse is really interesting to see.

## Seveneves (read)

Sort of split between the handling of an apocalyptic event and then a huge time skip to the far future of some very distinct post human splits of genetics.

## Children of Strife (read)

Maybe sort of a stretch but explores what happens after an apocalypse and terraforming new planets and what can go wrong in that process. A big time scale here versus other books typically thought of as post apocalyptic.
