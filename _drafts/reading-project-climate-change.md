Books that deal with the impending climate disaster, how to deal with it, and what comes next

## Transcendent by Stephen Baxter (read)

Book 3 in his Destiny's Children series and part of his Xeelee universe. Incredibly depressing but interesting read. Shows the world in the midst of handling the climate disaster in the 2040s just before things are going to be really bad for humanity. Shows a lot of the effects of climate change and transforms Florida into a battle against the coastline that humans are losing.

Has the thematic big ideas and radical moonshot ideas to try and deal with the climate emergency.

## Termination Shock (want to read)

Stephenson usually can be relied on to have wild ideas that are also possible and grounded in science.
